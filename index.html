02

How I think

Engineering principles that shape how I design and ship.

01

Design for failure.

Retries, checkpoints, idempotency and graceful degradation are part of the design—not afterthoughts.

02

Make complexity visible.

Good observability turns distributed systems from a black box into something a team can reason about.

03

Prefer boring reliability.

Simple architectures with clear ownership often outperform clever systems that are hard to operate.

04

Build for the next scale.

Think about data volume, concurrency, recovery and operational cost before the first production spike.
